# ❄️ Desnowing Datasets

This directory provides references and summaries of popular datasets used for snow removal from images in single and video desnowing tasks.

---

## 🏔️ Snow100K

Snow100K is a synthetic benchmark dataset with simulated snowy degradations.

* Total: 100,000 images
* 3 subsets: Snow100K-L, Snow100K-M, and Snow100K-S (light, medium, and strong snowfall)

> 📄 Dataset: [https://github.com/yxuan0525/Snow100K](https://github.com/yxuan0525/Snow100K)

---

## ❄️ CSD (Comprehensive Snow Dataset)

CSD includes a mixture of real and synthetic snowy images and has been used for training deep desnowing networks.

* \~13,000 images with a mix of dense and sparse snowfall conditions

> 📄 Source: [https://github.com/jiaya-git/DesnowNet](https://github.com/jiaya-git/DesnowNet)

---

## 🎥 Video Snow Removal Datasets

### RVD-Snow

RVD-Snow is a benchmark for real-world video desnowing tasks.

* Provides video sequences with snowfall of varying intensities and visibility

> 📄 Paper: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07299)
