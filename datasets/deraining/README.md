# 🌧️ Deraining Datasets

This directory includes datasets widely used for image and video rain removal (deraining) tasks across synthetic and real-world scenarios.

---

## ☔ Rain100H & Rain100L

These are two synthetic datasets commonly used for single-image deraining:

* **Rain100H**: 1,800 image pairs with heavy rain streaks and occlusions
* **Rain100L**: 200 image pairs with light and sparse rain streaks

> 📄 Project: [https://xueyangfu.github.io/projects/tip2017.html](https://xueyangfu.github.io/projects/tip2017.html)

---

## 🌆 Rain800

Contains 800 synthetic rain images with corresponding clean images, used widely for lightweight deraining benchmarks.

> 📄 Source: [https://github.com/hezhangsprinter/DID-MDN](https://github.com/hezhangsprinter/DID-MDN)

---

## 🏙️ RainCityscapes

Cityscapes-based rainy image dataset generated by adding synthetic rain to urban driving scenes.

* Resolution: 1920×1080
* Samples: \~5,000

> 📄 Project: [https://xiaoyonghu.com/project/derain.html](https://xiaoyonghu.com/project/derain.html)

---

## 📊 DID-Data

DID-Data is a synthetic dataset designed for progressive density-aware deraining. It contains:

* 12,000 training pairs at different rain densities
* 1,000 testing pairs

> 📄 Project: [https://github.com/hezhangsprinter/DID-MDN](https://github.com/hezhangsprinter/DID-MDN)

---

## 🎥 Video Deraining Datasets

### NTURain

A real-world video rain removal dataset composed of driving and outdoor scenes.

* Paired hazy/clean video sequences
* Various rain intensities and camera motions

> 📄 Paper: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09556)

### SPAC-S dataset

Stereo video dataset used for temporal consistency-aware video deraining.

> 📄 Paper: [https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content\_ECCV\_2018/html/Yinglong\_Wang\_Spatial\_Attentive\_Single\_ECCV\_2018\_paper.html](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Yinglong_Wang_Spatial_Attentive_Single_ECCV_2018_paper.html)
